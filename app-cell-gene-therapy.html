Who uses it

Gene therapy and mRNA developers, CAR-T and cell therapy teams, viral vector CDMOs and academic translational centres.

Partners in this workflow

Challenges we help solve

Challenge 1Accurate vector titre

Genome titre by qPCR varies with standard curves; regulators expect robust quantification.

Challenge 2Full vs empty capsids

Empty and partial capsids reduce potency and must be measured lot to lot.

Challenge 3GMP-grade inputs

Cytokines, growth factors and supplements need documentation fit for clinical manufacturing.

The workflow, step by step

  1. 1Production inputs

    GMP proteins for cell expansion; supplements for vector yield.

    Bio-Techne GMP proteins · CELLiST AAV supplement
  2. 2Formulation

    Encapsulate nucleic acids in LNPs at screening to GMP scale.

    Unchained Sun-series · Inscinstech assembly
  3. 3Titre & copy number

    Absolute genome titre, residual DNA, vector copy number.

    RainSure RS32 · DropDx dPCR
  4. 4Particle quality

    Size, concentration, full/empty and single-particle data.

    Unchained Stunner AF · Leprechaun
  5. 5Purity & identity

    Protein identity and purity of vectors and products.

    Bio-Techne Simple Western, Maurice
