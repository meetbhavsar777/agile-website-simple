Challenges we help solve

Challenge 1Ghost peaks and baseline drift

Gradient impurities from mobile phase interfere with low-level impurity peaks.

Challenge 2Hard-to-source impurity standards

Specified impurities for ANDA/DMF work are often unavailable or slow to import.

Challenge 3Column-to-column reproducibility

Methods must transfer between sites and survive column lot changes.

The workflow, step by step

  1. 1Method development

    Screen phases and pH to separate API from all specified impurities.

    Welch Ultisil®, Xtimate®, Boltimate®
  2. 2Reference standards

    Identify and quantify impurities against certified standards.

    TLC impurity standards
  3. 3Validation & transfer

    Prove specificity, linearity and robustness; transfer to QC.

    Welchrom® equivalents · TLC CoA
  4. 4Routine release & stability

    Run day-to-day testing with clean baselines.

    Ghost-Buster™ · Inscinstech HPLC
