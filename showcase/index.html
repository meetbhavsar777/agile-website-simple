Eleven ways to show our products

Every idea keeps the RainSure dPCR and adds the products from the document, HPLC columns included. Ideas 1–7 build on the home-page hero; 8–10 are new looks; 11 is the old website’s banner slider. Open one to see it full size.

1

Whole showcase switches

Every 5 seconds all five products change together: first the home instruments (RainSure dPCR stays), then the products from your document.
2

One product at a time

Every 4 seconds one product sinks away and a new one rises in its place, so the showcase keeps changing without ever being empty.
3

Spotlight

One big product on a large plinth with its name and a button. A new product comes every 4 seconds; the thumbnails let visitors jump.
4

Turntable

All products stand in a slow-turning circle. The one in front grows and shows its name.
5

By category

A list of categories on the left; the matching product appears on the right. Moves on every 4 seconds or on click.
6

Conveyor

A calm, endless row of products on plinths gliding across the page. Hover stops it.
7

Showcase, then spotlight

The five-product showcase first; then it steps aside and one big product takes the stage, a new one every 4 seconds, before the next showcase comes back. Made to look good on phones too.
8

Bento grid

A calm grid of tiles: the headline on a deep-blue tile with our figures, and one tile for each product family in the document, numbered in its order. The systems and instruments tiles change their picture by themselves.
9

Feature & quick links

The layout the big instrument makers use: a light, airy headline in a refined sans with an italic serif accent, one featured instrument changing every 5 seconds, and quick-link cards for the consumable families sitting across the edge of the hero.
10

Editorial

Set like a scientific journal: a masthead with our figures, a large serif headline, and three columns: an introduction, a captioned figure (Fig. 01, Fig. 02 …) and a contents list of the document’s product families, in order.
11

Old-site slider

Like the hero of the old agilescitech.in: full-width product banners and nothing else. The old site’s own banners plus new ones in the same style for RainSure, TLC, CELLiST, the S6000 and Bio-Techne research, all on one soft ice-blue background. On a phone each banner pans slowly so every label stays readable.
