Bioprocess & analytical systems

About Inscinstech

Inscinstech is a global innovator in automated bioprocess and analytical systems, delivering solutions that accelerate biologics, gene therapy, and RNA-based drug development.

The company’s technology portfolio covers the entire workflow — from upstream culture (bioreactors) to downstream purification (FPLC, TFF) and formulation (LNP assembly, oligonucleotide synthesis).

Each system integrates intelligent automation, modular hardware, and data-driven software to ensure reproducibility, scalability, and regulatory compliance (GLP / GMP / 21 CFR Part 11).

Designed for both R&D and pilot-scale GMP environments, Inscinstech instruments empower scientists to transition seamlessly from bench to process scale.

Molecular diagnostics

About RainSure

RainSure (RainSure Scientific) pioneers advanced molecular diagnostic and life science instrumentation by integrating microfluidics, precision optics, fluorescence detection, and intelligent software control. Their platforms are designed to deliver high sensitivity, multiplexing, and absolute quantification, while maintaining user-friendly workflows and compact footprints.

Their goal is to enable researchers, clinical scientists, and diagnostic labs to perform accurate nucleic acid analyses with speed, reliability, and efficiency.

Chromatography & separation

About Welch Materials

Welch Materials, Inc., founded in 2003, is an integrated chromatography and separation technology enterprise providing R&D, manufacturing, sales, and service for critical industries including biopharmaceuticals, food safety, fine chemicals and environmental monitoring.

Welch develops in-house columns and packing materials under brands such as Ultisil, Xtimate, Boltimate and Welchrom. The company holds 58 patents, with 19 column models listed in USP-PQRI database and 96 bonded phases in USP-ChromColumns; its columns are recommended in 13 test items in the 2025 Chinese Pharmacopoeia.

Flow imaging

About FlowCam

FlowCam rapidly acquires digital images of particles to measure particle size and morphology. Up to 50,000 particles/minute may be imaged in real time and analyzed based on more than 40 morphological parameters. Optional artificial intelligence tools for biopharmaceutical applications allow automated particle identification.
