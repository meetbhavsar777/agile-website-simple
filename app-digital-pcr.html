Challenges we help solve

Challenge 1qPCR variability

Standard curves add error and make results hard to compare between labs.

Challenge 2Rare targets in high background

Low-frequency mutations and trace DNA are below qPCR detection limits.

Challenge 3Hands-on time

Multi-instrument dPCR workflows need skilled operators and long setup.
