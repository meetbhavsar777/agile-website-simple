Home page · showcase ideas

Six ways to show our products

Each idea uses the same hero as the home page, with the RainSure dPCR kept and the products from the document added, HPLC columns included. Open one to see it full size.

1

Whole showcase switches

Every 5 seconds all five products change together: first the home instruments (RainSure dPCR stays), then the products from your document.
2

One product at a time

Every 4 seconds one product sinks away and a new one rises in its place, so the showcase keeps changing without ever being empty.
3

Spotlight

One big product on a large plinth with its name and a button. A new product comes every 4 seconds; the thumbnails let visitors jump.
4

Turntable

All products stand in a slow-turning circle. The one in front grows and shows its name.
5

By category

A list of categories on the left; the matching product appears on the right. Moves on every 4 seconds or on click.
6

Conveyor

A calm, endless row of products on plinths gliding across the page. Hover stops it.
